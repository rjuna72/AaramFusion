Originals are in assets/originals. Run `npm run images` to regenerate the five WebP sizes in public/images. No generative editing used. Cropping/compression uses Sharp. Central paths, alt text, credits and source URLs are in src/data/business.ts.

Replacement: obtain owner-authorised originals and photographer permission; replace originals and update central metadata/captions. The hero works best with a landscape breakfast spread, the coffee panel with a portrait or square close-up. Actual interior/team/gallery images remain a future content addition, not stock stand-ins.

Fonts: Fraunces and DM Sans are self-hosted via Fontsource packages. Package licenses are retained under node_modules/@fontsource; no visitor request to Google Fonts. The typographic wordmark is a proposed treatment, not a reproduced official mark.
