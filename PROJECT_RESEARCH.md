## 13. Website opportunities

1. Establish one coherent name, address and menu destination across pages.
2. Put menu and directions within easy reach on mobile.
3. Build a readable HTML menu preview rather than a PDF-only experience.
4. Explain availability uncertainty without creating unusable forms or booking buttons.
5. Use fast, local responsive imagery and minimal JavaScript.
6. Provide route-specific SEO and truthful Restaurant data; keep draft indexing disabled until owner checks and a real domain are configured.
7. Separate editable business data from page components.
